FAQ

Math tutoring questions families ask.

What math levels does Learning Squid tutor?

We tutor pre-algebra, algebra 1 and 2, geometry, trigonometry, precalculus, calculus (AB/BC), and the math sections of the SAT and ACT.

Is tutoring available in person or online?

Both. In-person sessions are available around the Seattle area, and online tutoring is available for families who prefer remote sessions.

How is project-based math tutoring different?

Alongside homework help and test preparation, students use the math they are learning in a small hands-on project or practical example so the concept has a reason to matter.
