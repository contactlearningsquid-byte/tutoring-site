A different kind of tutoring

Homework help is useful. Real understanding is better.

Traditional tutoring often stops once a student can finish the worksheet. Learning Squid goes one step further: we connect the academic concept to something the student can build, measure, test or explain themselves.

The goal isn't more schoolwork — it's schoolwork that finally feels connected to something real.

About Learning Squid

Built by an electrical engineer who loves teaching.

Learning Squid was founded by Logan Kilgore around a simple idea: students learn more deeply when tutoring connects the classroom to the real world.

Sessions use an engineering mindset — break a problem down, understand the fundamentals, test an idea and keep improving it — while staying focused on the student’s actual class, goals and interests.

Frequently asked

Questions families ask.

Do you offer tutoring in person or online?

Both. Learning Squid offers in-person tutoring around the Seattle area and online sessions for families who prefer remote learning.

What subjects does Learning Squid tutor?

We focus on math (algebra through calculus), physics, engineering and introductory programming, plus homework support, test preparation and study skills.

What makes Learning Squid different?

Sessions combine focused academic tutoring with hands-on projects that help students apply the concepts they are learning instead of only memorizing procedures.

Who teaches the sessions?

Learning Squid is currently founder-led. Sessions are taught by Logan Kilgore, an electrical engineer with a background in technical problem solving and one-on-one tutoring.
