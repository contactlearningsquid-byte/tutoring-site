About Clara

I've spent the last seven years teaching and tutoring math and science, including five years as a high school chemistry teacher. I started tutoring privately because one-on-one time is where students actually close their gaps — not in a classroom of thirty.

My approach is diagnostic first: before we touch homework, I find out exactly where a concept broke down, so we're not re-teaching things a student already understands.

Background
